================================================================================
WHAT THE BOT DOES
================================================================================

STRATEGY: Latency Arbitrage
- When BTC moves >0.3% on Binance, Polymarket lags 2-15 seconds
- Bot buys at old (mispriced) odds before market updates
- Expected win rate: 70-75%
- Expected ROI: 15-25% per trade

EXAMPLE:
1. BTC pumps +0.5% on Binance (detected instantly)
2. Polymarket still shows 50/50 odds (lagging)
3. Bot buys "UP" at 50¢
4. Polymarket updates to 75/25 a few seconds later
5. You have 73% win probability but only paid 50¢

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Max $10 per trade (configurable)
- Circuit breaker after 3 losses
- Only trades in minutes 2-12 of 15-min window
- Paper trading mode by default
